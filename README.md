## 🌟 Major Launch Platforms

> The big ones. High traffic, high signal. Launch here first.

- [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com) :: The #1 platform for launching new products. Get feedback, traction, and press coverage.
- [BetaList](https://betalist.com) :: Discover and get early access to tomorrow's startups. Great for pre-launch buzz.
- [Betapage](https://betapage.co) :: Discover and launch new startups daily.
- [Betabound](https://betabound.com) :: Beta testing community for new products.
- [Open Launch](https://open-launch.com) :: Open-source alternative to Product Hunt. Free and community-driven.
- [Launching Next](https://www.launchingnext.com) :: Curated list of upcoming product launches.
- [Openhunts](https://openhunts.com) :: Launch and hunt products openly.
- [PeerPush](https://peerpush.net) :: Get instant visibility for your product by people who care now.
- [Pitchwall](https://pitchwall.co) :: Premium startup showcase with curated placements.
- [ProductBurst](https://productburst.com) :: Daily featured products and startup showcases.
- [ProductLaunchpad](https://productlaunchpad.app) :: Launch your product to early adopters.
- [Promote Project](https://www.promoteproject.com) :: Platform for promoting and marketing your projects.
- [SellWithBoost](https://sellwithboost.com) :: Discover and launch startups, tools, and products.
- [Shipybara](https://shipybara.com) :: Launch your startup, get seen by early adopters.
- [Super Launch](https://www.superlaun.ch) :: Curated product launch platform and directory.
- [The Startup Pitch](https://thestartuppitch.com) :: Pitch your startup to investors and users.
- [TinyLaunch](https://www.tinylaunch.com) :: Product launch platform with high visibility.
- [TinyLaunchpad](https://tinylaunchpad.com) :: Fresh products and startups launched every Monday.
- [Turbo0](https://turbo0.com) :: Fast-track platform for launching products quickly.
- [HUNT0](https://hunt0.com) :: LaunchPad :: Ship Early. Hunt Early.
- [Launch Vibe](https://www.launchvibe.app) :: Launch to thousands of founders.
- [LaunchIgniter](https://launchigniter.com) :: Product launch platform with community voting.
- [Launchy.tools](https://launchy.tools) :: A weekly product launch platform, visual-first.
- [Aura++](https://auraplusplus.com) :: Increase your online aura with a product launch.
- [neeed.directory](https://neeed.directory) :: Discover and submit new tools and products.
- [startupfa.st](https://www.startupfa.st) :: Startup directory with launch exposure.
- [aat.ee](https://www.aat.ee) :: Product launches and discovery.
- [LaunchCaster](https://launchcaster.xyz) :: Web3-native product launches.
- [DiscoverLaunches](https://discoverlaunches.com) :: Weekly launch board for indie makers, with permanent product pages and dofollow links.

## 🧑‍💻 Developer & Open Source

> Built for developers. Ideal for dev tools, CLI apps, open-source projects, and APIs.

- [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com) :: Post a "Show HN" to get feedback from the tech world's most critical audience.
- [Dev.to](https://dev.to) :: Developer community and blogging platform. Write about your launch.
- [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com) :: Build reputation by answering questions, then share when relevant.
- [Lobsters](https://lobste.rs) :: Developer-focused tech news (invite-only, but high signal).
- [Open Alternative](https://openalternative.co) :: Open-source alternatives to popular software.
- [EuroAlternative](https://euroalternative.co) :: Discover European alternatives to big tech.
- [Alternative.me](https://alternative.me) :: Find alternatives to popular software and services.
- [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net) :: Crowdsourced software recommendations and alternatives.

## 📰 Tech Media & Press

> Get featured in major publications for massive reach. Harder to crack, but high impact.

- [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com) :: Leading technology media. Hard to get featured but massive impact.
- [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com) :: Tech news and analysis. Data-driven stories work well.
- [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com) :: Technology, science, art, and culture news.
- [Wired](https://www.wired.com) :: Deep tech and future-focused feature stories.
- [Mashable](https://mashable.com) :: Digital culture and tech news. High viral potential.
- [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com) :: Business, innovation, and design magazine.
- [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com) :: Business and finance publication.
- [Inc. Magazine](https://www.inc.com) :: Entrepreneur and small business focused.
- [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com) :: Business, tech, and finance news.
- [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com) :: Magazine for entrepreneurs and business owners.
- [Medium](https://medium.com) :: Publishing platform with a built-in audience. Submit to publications.
- [Slashdot](https://slashdot.org) :: Tech community news. Technical angle is key.
- [Designer News](https://www.designernews.co) :: Community for designers. Share design-focused insights.

## 💡 Startup Communities

> Join the conversation. Where founders hang out, share, and support each other.

**Reddit Communities**
- [Reddit – r/startups](https://www.reddit.com/r/startups) :: Large community of entrepreneurs. Follow rules strictly.
- [Reddit – r/entrepreneur](https://www.reddit.com/r/entrepreneur) :: Entrepreneur community on Reddit.
- [Reddit – r/SaaS](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaaS) :: SaaS-focused Reddit community.
- [Reddit – r/webdev](https://www.reddit.com/r/webdev) :: Web development community.

**Maker Communities**
- [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com) :: Community of founders sharing strategies and revenue numbers.
- [Makerlog](https://getmakerlog.com) :: Build in public with a supportive community.

- [Side Project Stack](https://sideprojectstack.com) :: Showcase your product and tech stack.
- [SideProjectors](https://www.sideprojectors.com) :: Promote or even sell your side project.
- [Indie Deals](https://www.indie.deals) :: Deals and discounts for indie makers and startups.
- [Growth Hackers](https://growthhackers.com) :: Community for growth marketing professionals.
- [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io) :: Professional network for builders, developers, and makers.

**Platforms & Communities**
- [F6S](https://www.f6s.com) :: Global startup community with access to funding programs.
- [Quora](https://www.quora.com) :: Q&A platform with topic-specific spaces. Answer relevant questions.
